ⓘ   About the Metrics

SIP: An installment at the chosen frequency, starting from your chosen Investment Month/Day, running for one year (or up to the last available NAV, for the current year), valued one year later — shown as the actual gain on total invested.

Lumpsum: A one-time investment on your chosen Investment Month/Day, valued exactly one year later — shown as the actual (non-annualised) gain for that window.

SIP Day of Month / Investment Day: change these to move the whole 1-year window — e.g. Investment Month=April measures Apr-to-Apr (financial year) instead of Jan-Dec.

Return % =   ( End Value / Total Invested − 1 )   ×  100
Reported per 1-year window, not annualised.
